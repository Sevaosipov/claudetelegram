# EURUSD swing trades: the result (2026-10-11)

Plan: PREREGISTRATION_SWING_EURUSD.md. Design period 2012-2019: 88,872 hourly bars in all, 7,117 setups, 240
candidates, one trade at a time.

**Verdict: no rule found.** 7 of the 240 made any money in eight years; none had a profit in 6 of the 8 years.
Nothing was chosen, so the hold-out (2020-2026) was not run.

- The median candidate lost 0.12R a trade. The best with 100 trades or more -- OPEN-RANGE, stop at the far side,
  with the trend, out at 5R or after 14 days -- made +0.041R a trade over 571 trades, with five losing years of
  eight, the last four all down.
- Holding longer made things worse, not better: with the entries' own (near) stops the nightly financing is a large
  part of the risk, and the no-target, 28-day variants lost about 0.5R a trade.
- Widening the stop to half a day's range cut the financing but not the loss: the entries do not predict the next
  days any better than the next hours.

With REPORT_DAY_EURUSD.md this makes 528 variants of the same three entries -- by the day, by the week, tight or wide,
filtered or not. The entries have no edge on EURUSD; how a trade is managed afterwards cannot add one.
