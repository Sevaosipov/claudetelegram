# TPR across eight markets: the result (2026-10-12)

Plan: PREREGISTRATION_TPR.md. Seven currency pairs and gold, one-hour and four-hour bars, 2012-2019, 48 candidates
judged on all markets together.

**No rule found: none of the 48 made money.** The least bad -- four-hour bars, entry at 61.8 %, stop at point 0, out
at A -- lost 0.025R a trade on 1,289 trades: three years of eight up, four markets of eight up. It wins 40 % of
its trades where a coin toss with the same stop and target wins 38 %; the difference does not cover the costs.
Asking for an 8-ATR move, trading only with the trend, or aiming for the 161.8 % line each made it worse. The
hold-out (2020 on) was not run.

**A correction.** The first run of this test showed a profit (+0.07R a trade, t 3.6). It came from an error in the
test, found before anything was concluded from it: when one bar fell through the entry and through point 0, the
setup was dropped as «lapsed» instead of counted as a filled trade stopped at a loss. With that fixed the profit is
gone. The same error flattered the two EURUSD rounds of REPORT_ABC.md; they were negative even so.
