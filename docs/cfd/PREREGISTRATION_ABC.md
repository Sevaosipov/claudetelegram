# The 0-A-B-C «sequence» on EURUSD: the plan

Written 2026-10-12, before any run. The user sent a two-page sheet («Sequence Validity»): a move from an absolute
high or low (point 0) to a sub-extreme (A); once price has retraced past 38.2 % of it the two points are confirmed;
the entry is at the 50-66.7 % retracement; targets are A again, then the 100 % and 161-200 % extensions; the stop
is tight and goes to break-even. It gives no statistics. Written as rules (long; a short mirrors it):

- **Point 0:** a bar's low that is the lowest of the last 96 fifteen-minute bars (a day). **A:** the highest high
  since. Confirmed at the first bar whose low is at or under A - 0.382 x (A - 0), if A - 0 is at least M x ATR(14).
  A new high cancels and re-forms it; a lower low starts over.
- **Entry:** a limit at the E retracement of 0-A, filled from the bar after the confirmation on.
- **Stop:** at point 0, or at the 78.6 % retracement.
- **Exit:** everything at A; or thirds at A, at entry + (A - 0) and at entry + 1.618 x (A - 0), the stop to the
  entry after the first and to A after the second. No time exit.
- Costs 0.8 pips and 0.010 % a night (IC Markets Raw); no trade when the cost is over 0.25R; one trade at a time a
  side.
- **Candidates (16):** M 4 or 8; E 50 % or 61.8 %; the two stops; the two exits.
- **Choice** on 2021-2024 (FXCM one-minute candles as 15-minute bars): at least 150 trades, 3 of 4 years up, t at
  least 2.5; the highest t. None: «no rule found».
- **Verdict:** the chosen one, once, on 2025-01-01 on: sum above zero and both years above zero.
